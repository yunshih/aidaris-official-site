Who we are

AIDARIS (艾達睿思) operates aidaris.com. Office: 9 F.-1, No. 21, Wufu 3rd Rd., Qianjin Dist., Kaohsiung City 801005, Taiwan (R.O.C.). Contact: contact@aidaris.com.

Information we collect on this website

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage (pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location). If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send. We do not sell personal information.

Google user data in our internal tools

AIDARIS operates internal productivity tools that, with the explicit consent of our own staff, access Google Workspace data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive) through Google APIs. These tools are used only by AIDARIS staff for their own accounts and are not offered to the public.

Data accessed through Google APIs is used solely to provide user-facing features to the consenting staff member, such as summarizing email, preparing draft replies, and checking calendar availability. We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide these features, to comply with law, or with the user's consent.

AIDARIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention and security

OAuth tokens and any working data are stored on company-controlled devices with restricted access and are deleted when no longer needed or when access is revoked.
